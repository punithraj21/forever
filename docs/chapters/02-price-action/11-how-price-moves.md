# How Price Moves

> **In one line:** Price does not move in a straight line — it moves in waves, in a repeating cycle of a strong push and a small pullback.

## 🎯 What you'll learn
- Why price moves in waves, not in a straight line.
- The battle between buyers and sellers that pushes price.
- What an impulse, a correction, and a consolidation are.
- How waves make an uptrend or a downtrend.

## 📘 Key concepts

### Price is a tug-of-war
Think of a bazaar (market). At every moment, buyers and sellers are fighting over one price.

- **Buyers (demand — how much people want to buy)** push the price **up**.
- **Sellers (supply — how much people want to sell)** push the price **down**.
- When neither side wins, price moves **sideways** (flat, no clear direction).

The simple flow to remember:

- **Buyers (demand) → Price moves (up, down, sideways) → Sellers (supply).**

### Price moves in waves
Price never goes straight up like a rocket. It moves in small steps, like a person climbing stairs — up, small rest, up again.

- **Impulse (a strong, fast move in the trend's direction)** — the big push.
- **Correction / Pullback (a slower, smaller move against the impulse)** — the small rest.
- One impulse plus one pullback = one wave. Then it repeats.

This repeating up-push and down-rest is why we say price moves in a **cycle**, not by luck.

### Waves build trends
The **highs** (peaks) and **lows** (bottoms) of these waves tell you the trend.

- **Higher High + Higher Low = Uptrend** — each peak is higher, each dip is higher. Price is climbing.
- **Lower High + Lower Low = Downtrend** — each peak is lower, each dip is lower. Price is falling.
- If highs and lows are flat and equal, price is in a **range** (sideways).

### What drives the waves
Many things push buyers and sellers at once:

- **Liquidity (how easily orders get filled)** — fuel for the move.
- **News and economic data** — create sudden **volatility (fast, big price swings)**.
- **Market sentiment (the crowd's mood — fear or greed)** — changes how people react.
- **Technical levels** like **support (a floor where buyers step in)** and **resistance (a ceiling where sellers step in)**.

## 🔍 Example
Imagine a share of "ABC Ltd" in an uptrend.

- It rises from ₹100 to ₹120 → this is the **impulse** (strong push up).
- It dips from ₹120 to ₹110 → this is the **pullback** (small rest, not a full drop).
- It rises again from ₹110 to ₹135 → next **impulse**.

Look at the numbers: the high went 120 → 135 (higher high), and the low went 100 → 110 (higher low). That is an **uptrend**.

```
       135
   120  /
   /\  /
  /  \/  <- pullback to 110
 /
100
```

The path looks like stairs going up, not a straight line.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes
- Thinking price will go up in a straight line — it always rests and pulls back.
- Panicking during a normal pullback and selling too early.
- Buying at the very top of an impulse, right before a pullback.
- Believing price moves are random — they follow a cycle of impulse and correction.
- Ignoring the highs and lows, so you miss whether the trend is up or down.

## ✅ Key takeaways
- Price moves in waves, not in a straight line, and it is not random.
- Each wave = one impulse (strong push) + one correction (small pullback).
- Higher High + Higher Low = uptrend; Lower High + Lower Low = downtrend.
- Buyers push price up, sellers push price down, and both fight all the time.
- Sideways movement (consolidation) builds energy for the next big move.

## 📝 Quick check
1. **Q:** Does price move in a straight line?
   **A:** No. It moves in waves — a strong push (impulse), then a small rest (pullback), again and again.
2. **Q:** What pattern of highs and lows means an uptrend?
   **A:** Higher highs and higher lows — each peak and each dip is higher than the last.
3. **Q:** What is a pullback?
   **A:** The correction — a smaller move against the trend, between two impulses.

## 📖 New words
- **Demand** — how much buyers want to buy; strong demand pushes price up.
- **Supply** — how much sellers want to sell; strong supply pushes price down.
- **Impulse** — a strong, fast move in the direction of the trend.
- **Correction / Pullback** — a slower, smaller move against the impulse.
- **Consolidation** — sideways movement that builds energy for the next move.
- **Higher High** — a peak that is higher than the previous peak.
- **Higher Low** — a dip that is higher than the previous dip.
- **Uptrend** — price rising, making higher highs and higher lows.
- **Downtrend** — price falling, making lower highs and lower lows.
- **Volatility** — how fast and how big price swings are.
- **Liquidity** — how easily buy and sell orders get filled.
- **Support** — a price floor where buyers tend to step in.
- **Resistance** — a price ceiling where sellers tend to step in.
- **Market sentiment** — the crowd's overall mood, fear or greed.
